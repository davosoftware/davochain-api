# Order Precision

We are fully committed to ensuring that transactions can run efficiently and provide value to everyone who needs it. To this effect, we are in the process of making a couple of changes. These changes include:

* Minimum order value: On our end, the minimum order value will be 1000 Naira for NGN or 90 cents ($0.9) for USDT. Any user transactions below 1000 Naira or 90 cents ($0.9) will likely throw an error on your end.

**Recommended action**: Update the minimum transaction amount your users can enter.

* Our precision values for trading pairs are changing. This affects the number of decimal places each currency in the pair can have in terms of volume and price.

Here’s an example:

For the BTC/USDT pair, BTC will have a precision of 5 (maximum of 5 figures after the decimal), USDT will have a precision of 2 (maximum of 2 figures after the decimal) and the pair's price will have a precision of 2.
