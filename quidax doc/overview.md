# Getting Started

## Sign up

![](https://files.readme.io/60126ec-Getting_Started.jpg)

To get started, you have to create an account on the [sign-up page](https://pro.quidax.io/en_US/register) by filling in your accurate details, - Business Name, Username, First Name, Last Name, E-mail Address, Password, and Phone Number. Click the buisness section on the page when registering for an account
